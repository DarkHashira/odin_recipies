Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish featuring layers of wide, flat pasta, typically baked with a rich filling and sauce. The layers usually include meat (like ground beef or sausage), vegetables, ricotta cheese, mozzarella, and a tomato-based sauce or a béchamel sauce. It's often topped with grated Parmesan cheese and baked until golden brown and bubbly.

Steps

  1. Boil lasagna sheets.
  2. Prepare meat or veggie sauce.
  3. Make white sauce (béchamel).
  4. Layer sauce, sheets, and cheese.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30–40 mins.
