BIRIYANI

Biryani is a flavorful, aromatic South Asian rice dish made with spices, rice, and meat, fish, eggs, or vegetables. It's known for its layered construction, with fragrant basmati rice layered with marinated meat or vegetables, and often includes fried onions, nuts, and fresh herbs. The dish is traditionally cooked using the "dum pukht" method, where it's slow-cooked over low heat to allow the flavors to meld together.

steps

  1. Soak rice and marinate meat with spices.
  2. Fry onions till golden brown.
  3. Cook meat until tender.
  4. Layer rice and meat with fried onions.
  5. Cover and cook on low flame (dum).
