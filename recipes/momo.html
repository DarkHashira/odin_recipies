MOMO

Momos are steamed or fried dumplings, popular in Tibetan, Nepali, and other South Asian cuisines. They typically consist of a dough wrapper filled with savory ingredients like minced meat or vegetables. Momos are often served with a spicy dipping sauce or soup.

Steps

  1. Prepare dough using flour and water.
  2. Make stuffing with minced meat or veggies.
  3. Roll dough into small circles.
  4. Fill and fold to form momos.
  5. Steam for 10–15 minutes.
